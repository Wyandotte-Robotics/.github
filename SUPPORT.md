# Getting help

- **Team members:** ask a programming lead or mentor, or open an issue in the repository you're working in.
- **Other teams:** questions about our code are welcome. Open an issue, or reach us through [wyandotterobotics.com/contact](https://wyandotterobotics.com/contact).
- **Families, sponsors and everyone else:** visit [wyandotterobotics.com](https://wyandotterobotics.com) or email hello@wyandotterobotics.com.
