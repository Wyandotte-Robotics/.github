# Code of Conduct

Wyandotte Robotics is a student program of Wyandotte Public Schools. Everyone who takes part here, including students, coaches, mentors, parents and visitors, agrees to this code in our repositories, issues, pull requests and discussions.

## What we expect

We follow the *FIRST* Core Values: **Discovery, Innovation, Impact, Inclusion, Teamwork and Fun**, and practice Gracious Professionalism®.

In practice that means:

- Be kind and patient, especially with people who are new or still learning.
- Critique code, not people. Explain what to change and why.
- Give credit for other people's work and ideas.
- Respect everyone's privacy. Never post anyone's personal information or photos.
- Help teammates and other teams succeed. Competing hard and being helpful go together.

## What isn't allowed

- Harassment, bullying, insults or name-calling
- Discriminatory jokes or language about anyone's race, ethnicity, religion, gender, sexual orientation, disability, age or appearance
- Sexual language or imagery
- Posting someone's personal information without their permission
- Deliberately breaking other people's work

District policies and the student code of conduct apply here the same as at school or at a competition.

## Reporting a problem

If something here makes you uncomfortable, tell a coach in person or email **hello@wyandotterobotics.com**. Reports are kept as private as possible.

Coaches may remove comments, close issues, or remove anyone from the organization who doesn't follow this code, and will follow district procedures where they apply.
